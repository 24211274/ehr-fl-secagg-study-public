## Controlled files

The controlled repository's [availability guide](https://github.com/24211274/ehr-fl-secagg-study/blob/study-artifacts-v1.30/docs/AVAILABILITY.md) lists all four packages and their verification commands. A private URL does not grant read permission. Contact the repository owner through the normal supervisor/research access process.

The private archive includes all required recorded inputs and selected models. Historical replay scripts retain machine-specific paths; their inclusion is not a new independent-host training validation. Raw-data generation, provenance and redistribution rights remain unresolved. Public access to code and aggregate results grants no dataset licence or ethics approval.

The public subset intentionally excludes original membership/draw records, the embedded schema resource, learned tensors, fitted schemas/scalers and historical archives with record-level or editorial material. These exclusions are listed by path in [PUBLIC_SELECTION.json](../evidence/PUBLIC_SELECTION.json). Full evidence remains in the controlled archive. The author's declaration identifier is withheld in the publication thesis; the scientific results are unchanged.
